## 观点表达

### 赞同

**I think so, I suppose so, I guess so.**

这三个用来表达一定程度上的赞同。

Ex.

Question: Has autumn come in your city?
Answer: I guess so, cause I can see the leaves are falling down On the streets.

Question: And do you like autumn?
Answer: I think so. It's a cozy season.

**That makes sense to me.**

表示某种程度的同意或支持。

Ex.

Question: Do you think that reading book is more beneficial than watching TV.
Answer: That makes sense to me, cause reading a book can broaden my horizons And enhance my critical thinking, but watching TV will be more passive.

**That is very true,**

表示完全的支持

Ex.

Question: Do you believe that learning a second language is beneficial for personal growth?
Answer: That is very true. Learning a second language opens up new opportunities And can help you get familiar with other countries students to break down the gap between countries.

**Absolutely, definitely 和 exactly**

表达一种轻快的简短的统一，可以直接跟上后面的原因。

Ex.

Question: Do you think that volunteering is beneficial for the personal development?
Answer: Definitely volunteering It can not only help others but also gives you a chance to communicate with other volunteers and gain a very meaningful experience.
