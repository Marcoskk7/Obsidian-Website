### 动态图
概述段：

趋势简单的时候，可以一句话概括所有折线的趋势，另1-2句对比三条线的数据变化幅度和速度
趋势复杂的时候，把具体每条线的趋势呈现放到正文段，概述重点对比变化幅度、极值等内容

正文段：

1．趋势复杂的时候，按照线的顺序分段，在正段都要按照时间顺序，把每条线的趋势、变化、特殊值依次皇现，主要使叙述，可以不或少对
2.趋势简单的时候，按照时间顺序分段，把时间分成2段，每个正段描写1个时间段内多条折线的数据变化，主要使对，可以不或少描述
3.当折线数量非常多的时候（比如有5条折线，这类题目很少），按照线的顺序分段，比如个正段写2条趋势相近的折线，另一段写其他3条趋势相近的线，主要使用对比，可以不用或少用描述

### 第一段结构: 背景+点题+开门见山

背景:
In modern epoch, the issue of xxx has always been a matter of public interest
比如说第一题, 可以填写 housework, 也可以填写 children's education

点题:
观点类: 
Agree: Some individuals champion the notion that xxx.
Discuss: Some individuals champion the notion that xxx, while a significance number of others posit that...

现象类:
It could be obviously witnessed a phenomenon that ...

开门见山:

Agree: Despite recognizing the merits and drawbacks behind the viewpoint, I am inclined to support/oppose it.
Discuss: Despite recognizing the validities of both sides, I am inclined to support the former/latter.
优缺点: Despite recognizing the merits and drawbacks behind the phenomenon, I am inclined to believe it has more advantages/disadvantages.
2 tasks: This essay aims to delve into the analysis of the causes and potential influences behind the phenomenon


### 结尾:
观点类:
In conclusion, I remain unwavering in my conviction that making plans for the future proves to be useless. While potential pitfalls lurk in the backdrop, the arguments I have elucidated above robustly vindicate my stance.

优缺点类:
In conclusion, I remain unwavering in my conviction that the advantages carry more weight. While potential pitfalls lurk in the backdrop, the arguments I have elucidated above robustly vindicate my stance.

2 tasks 类:
In conclusion, the dilemma of xxx is multifaceted and intricate issue with an array of underlying reasons and wide-ranging consequences. It is imperative to acknowledge that it is caused by xxxx and may bring the effects of xxxx.

解决方式类:
In conclusion, while the dilemma of xxx is incontrovertibly complex and multi-dimensional, it is still imperative to acknowledge that it is caused by xxxx and may be solved through xxxx.
